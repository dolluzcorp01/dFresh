dFresh Back Office - test from scratch, page by page
The order to set up and run dFresh from day one. Each step needs the steps before it.
Page = where to go in the left menu.  Do = what to enter.  Check = what should happen.

Before you start
  - Use dFresh_Back_Office_Empty.html: it opens with no business data. Staff, roles, approval
    rules, switches, numbering, the company profile and the godowns are already there.
  - The eraser button in the header (Clear data) empties everything again at any time, so you
    can test, clear and test again. Numbers start again from 1.
  - The Tests page "Start again" button loads the sample data instead (for the written tests).
  - Log in as Shoban B (Super admin) unless a step says otherwise. Switch person with the
    "who" box at the top.
  - Things that need approval: approve them as a second person (Pavithran V V or Anitha R)
    in Home > Approvals before going on.


PHASE 1 - THE COMPANY (Admin)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Admin > Organisation > Company
      Do    : company name, GSTIN, PAN, address, bank account, UPI ID.
      Check : bank account and UPI wait for a second Admin. Approve as Pavithran.

 2. Admin > Organisation > Godowns
      Do    : add your godown(s) and the person in charge of each.
      Check : each godown has its own code (used in document numbers).

 3. Admin > Organisation > Numbering
      Do    : check the prefixes for invoice, order, PO, return, etc.
      Check : the sample number shown looks right.

 4. Admin > Users and security > Roles
      Do    : look at each role (Sales, Warehouse, Supervisor...) and what it can do.
      Check : giving a role wider rights waits for a second Admin.

 5. Admin > Users and security > Staff
      Do    : add staff, give each a role, godowns and an approval limit.
      Check : anyone who can sign waits for a second Admin before they are active.

 6. Admin > Users and security > Stand-ins
      Do    : set who approves when a supervisor is on leave.
      Check : a stand-in must already approve the same things at the same limit.

 7. Admin > Users and security > Approval rules
      Do    : check each rule and its limit (discount, PO, write-off, expense...).
      Check : loosening a rule waits for a second Admin; bank rules cannot be switched off.

 8. Admin > Settings > Switches
      Do    : turn on the features you will test (QC hold, wallet, split shipments, e-way bill).
      Check : switching off a control waits for a second Admin.

 9. Admin > Communications > Notifications and Message templates
      Do    : check which events send messages and their wording.
      Check : nothing is really sent; messages go to the Outbox only.


PHASE 2 - THE BASE LISTS (Masters)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. Masters > Tax and units > Units of measure
      Do    : pack, carton, kg, roll and how they convert.

11. Masters > Tax and units > HSN and GST rates
      Do    : HSN code and GST % for your product groups.
      Check : a rate change from a date needs approval.

12. Masters > Catalogue > Categories
      Do    : Napkins, Rolls, Tissues... in the order you want.

13. Masters > Catalogue > Raw materials
      Do    : paper reels, cores, film, cartons.

14. Masters > Catalogue > Products and SKUs
      Do    : a new product, its pack sizes (SKUs), HSN, MRP, standard cost, reorder level.
      Check : the product stays Draft until made Active; draft packs cannot be sold.

15. Masters > Catalogue > Barcodes and labels
      Do    : link barcodes to the packs and cartons; print a label.

16. Masters > Catalogue > Kits (only if you sell kits)
      Do    : packs sold together as one.

17. Sales > Customers > Customer types
      Do    : shop, hotel, distributor... and their payment days.

18. Masters > Prices > Price lists
      Do    : price of each pack per list; the lowest allowed price.
      Check : a price below cost plus margin waits for approval.

19. Masters > Prices > Quantity slabs
      Do    : lower price for bigger quantity.

20. Masters > Partners > Suppliers
      Do    : a new supplier with GSTIN, MSME, bank account.
      Check : the bank account waits for approval; no payment until it is approved.

21. Masters > Partners > Vehicles and drivers, and Couriers
      Do    : your vehicle, driver, insurance and fitness dates; a courier.

22. Masters > Tax and units > Delivery areas
      Do    : towns and PIN codes you deliver to, days and charges.


PHASE 3 - BUY AND RECEIVE (Purchase)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
23. Purchase > Indents
      Do    : a staff request for raw material; turn it into a PO.

24. Purchase > Purchase orders
      Do    : PO to your supplier with price and expected date.
      Check : above the limit it waits for approval (orders to one supplier count together).

25. Purchase > Goods in
      Do    : record what arrived against the PO.
      Check : the goods go to QC hold, not to stock yet.

26. Purchase > Quality check
      Do    : accept the good ones, reject a few.
      Check : accepted goods go into stock; average cost is set; rejects become a claim.

27. Purchase > Supplier bills
      Do    : enter the supplier's bill for the goods in.
      Check : a price different from the PO waits for approval.

28. Purchase > Claims and purchase returns
      Do    : send the rejected goods back; record the supplier's credit note.

29. Money > Payments > Supplier payments
      Do    : pay the bill.
      Check : only to the approved bank account; big payments need a second person.


PHASE 4 - MAKE (Production) - skip if you only trade
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
30. Production > Production runs > Machines tab
      Do    : add a machine at your godown and tick the categories it makes.
      Check : a run can only be planned on a machine that makes that category.

    Production > Recipes
      Do    : raw material per pack and wastage % allowed.

31. Production > Production runs
      Do    : plan a run, start it (material is issued), finish it.
      Check : packs go to QC hold.

32. Purchase > Quality check (production runs)
      Do    : pass most packs, reject some.
      Check : passed packs go to stock at the made cost; rejects above the wastage %
              need approval.

33. Production > Wastage
      Check : wastage above the recipe is listed with its reason.


PHASE 5 - STOCK (Inventory)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
34. Inventory > Stock status
      Check : your new pack shows total, held and free at your godown.

35. Inventory > Batches and expiry
      Check : each batch with its date; expired ones are blocked.

36. Inventory > Transfers (if you have two godowns)
      Do    : send stock to the other godown; receive it there (try a short one).
      Check : a short goes to the Disputed shelf until Found or Lost is approved.

37. Inventory > Reorder
      Check : items below reorder level are listed.


PHASE 6 - SELL (Sales)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
38. Sales > Leads > Enquiries
      Do    : take an enquiry (from the website form or add one).

39. Sales > Leads > Follow-ups
      Do    : set a call-back date and a note.

40. Sales > Customers > Customer list
      Do    : turn the enquiry into a customer: type, godown, salesperson, address.

41. Sales > Customers > Credit limits
      Do    : give the customer a credit limit (or leave as cash).

42. Masters > Prices > Special prices (optional)
      Do    : one agreed price for this customer.
      Check : below the allowed limit it waits for approval.

43. Marketing > Offers, Coupons, Cashback rules (optional)
      Do    : an offer or a cashback rule.
      Check : a cashback rule goes live only after approval.

44. Sales > Orders > Quotes
      Do    : a quote for the customer; send it; turn it into an order.

45. Sales > Orders > Orders
      Do    : confirm the order.
      Check : the stock is held for this customer; big discounts wait for approval.

46. Sales > Orders > Back-orders (if stock was short)
      Check : the short items are owed and held as soon as stock arrives.

47. Sales > Orders > Standing orders (optional)
      Do    : a repeat order every week.


PHASE 7 - PACK AND DELIVER (Fulfilment)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
48. Fulfilment > Packing > To pick
      Do    : print or follow the pick list.

49. Fulfilment > Packing > Packing
      Do    : scan the packs into boxes; press Packed.
      Check : stock goes down; an expired or wrong batch is refused.

50. Money > Billing > Invoices
      Do    : make the invoice for the packed order (before the goods leave).
      Check : above the limit it asks for the e-way bill number.

51. GST > E-way bill / E-invoice
      Do    : add the e-way bill number (and the IRN if e-invoice is on).

52. Fulfilment > Packing > Packed, waiting
      Check : the order is ready to load.

53. Fulfilment > Delivery > Trips (or Courier and pickup)
      Do    : load the order on a trip with a vehicle and driver; send the trip.

54. Fulfilment > Delivery > Proof of delivery
      Do    : record the delivery with a signature or photo.

55. Fulfilment > Delivery > Failed / short / refused (try one)
      Check : packs not kept come back to stock and a credit note is made.

56. Fulfilment > Delivery > Cash on delivery
      Do    : record the cash the driver hands in; close the trip.


PHASE 8 - GET PAID (Money)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
57. Money > Payments > Receipts
      Do    : record the customer's payment (UPI, bank, cheque or cash).
      Check : the invoice shows paid; the customer ledger is up to date.

58. Sales > Customers > Customer ledger
      Check : invoice, payment and balance are all there.

59. Money > Billing > Debit notes (optional)
      Do    : an extra charge on an invoice.

60. Money > Payments > Refunds (optional)
      Do    : pay money back to a customer.
      Check : needs approval.


PHASE 9 - RETURNS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
61. Sales > Returns > Return requests
      Do    : the customer sends some packs back.
      Check : outside the return window it needs approval.

62. Sales > Returns > Inspection
      Do    : decide each pack: back to stock, repack or scrap.

63. Money > Billing > Credit notes
      Check : the credit note is made from the return; the customer ledger goes down.

64. Marketing > Customer wallets (if wallet is on)
      Check : cashback earned on the invoice; part taken back after the return.


PHASE 10 - EXPENSES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
65. Money > Expenses > Petty cash
      Do    : top up the godown cash box.

66. Money > Expenses > Expenses
      Do    : rent, fuel or a small spend; try a new payee.
      Check : a new payee is approved once; big amounts need approval.


PHASE 11 - CHECK AND CLOSE THE MONTH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
67. Inventory > Stock take
      Do    : count a shelf; enter a difference.
      Check : the difference is posted only after approval.

68. Inventory > Write-offs
      Do    : write off a damaged pack.
      Check : needs approval; stock and value go down.

69. Inventory > Stock ledger
      Check : every movement of your pack from goods in to delivery is there.

70. Home > Dashboard and Home > Alerts
      Check : today's sales, stock, money owed and alerts match what you did.

71. Reports > Sales and purchases, Stock, Money owed, GST and delivery
      Check : the numbers agree with the pages you used.

72. GST > GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B data
      Check : the month's sales, credit notes and tax by rate.

73. Money > Accounting export > Export to accountant
      Do    : export a FINISHED month to the CA (the running month is refused;
              a Super admin test tool can treat October as ended).
      Check : the month is locked; nothing dated in it can change without approval.

74. Money > Accounting export > Export history
      Check : what was sent, when and by whom.

75. Admin > Users and security > Audit log, and Admin > Communications > Outbox
      Check : every change you made is logged; every message is in the Outbox.


Not in this order (not built yet or later)
