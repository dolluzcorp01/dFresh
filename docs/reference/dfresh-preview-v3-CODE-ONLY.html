About us

A gentle brand from Kanchipuram.

dFresh is a tissue and hygiene paper brand of Dolluz Corporation (OPC) Pvt Ltd. From our godown in Kanchipuram we supply homes, shops, hotels, restaurants, offices and hospitals across the Kanchipuram, Vellore and Chennai region.

🌿Our promise

Paper that feels gentle, packed fresh and priced fairly for the people who use it every day.
